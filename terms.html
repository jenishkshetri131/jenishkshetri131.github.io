StudyPilot Terms of Service

Last updated: 12 October 2026

StudyPilot is an academic prototype built for an MSc Artificial Intelligence module at the University of East London. It is a student project, not a commercial service.

1. About the service

StudyPilot is an AI assistant that helps students explore overseas study options on WhatsApp. It can collect a study profile, suggest countries, estimate admission chances, send follow-up messages, and help book an appointment with a consultant.

2. Guidance, not advice or guarantees

  • Country suggestions and admission-chance estimates are automated guidance based on the information you give. They are not a promise, offer, or guarantee of admission or of a visa.
  • Information about universities, fees, and visas can change. Always check official university and government sources before making decisions.
  • StudyPilot is not legal, immigration, or financial advice.

3. Using the service

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • Give accurate information and do not misuse the service.
  • You may ask for a human consultant at any time.

4. Messages and opt-out

By messaging StudyPilot you agree to receive replies and, if you opt in, follow-up and reminder messages. Reply STOP at any time to stop follow-up messages.
