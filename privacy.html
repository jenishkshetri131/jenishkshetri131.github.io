StudyPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

StudyPilot is an academic prototype built for an MSc Artificial Intelligence module at the University of East London. It is a student project and is not a commercial service. It is tested with test accounts and synthetic data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Jenish Kshetri, the student developer of StudyPilot. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What StudyPilot does

StudyPilot is an assistant for an overseas education consultancy. It talks to students on WhatsApp, collects a study profile, suggests suitable countries, gives an explainable estimate of admission chances, sends follow-up messages, and helps book a consultant appointment.

3. What data we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and display name, and the messages you send us.
  • Study profile details you choose to share: target country, course, budget, grades, English test score, intake, and work experience.
  • Appointment details (date, time, consultant).
  • Your consent and opt-out choices, with the date they were recorded.

We ask only for what is needed to give study advice. Please do not send passport numbers, identity documents, or other sensitive information in chat.

4. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To answer your questions and give study recommendations: your consent.
  • To send follow-up and reminder messages: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying STOP.
  • To book and manage appointments: your consent.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. To run the service, data may be processed by:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), which carries the messages.
  • A language model provider, which generates replies.
  • Google Calendar, which stores appointments.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

6. Automated processing

Replies are generated by an AI assistant, and the admission-chance estimate comes from a scoring model. The estimate is guidance only, not a guarantee or an offer. You can ask for a human consultant at any time, and a person reviews cases the assistant cannot handle confidently.

7. How long we keep data

Conversation and profile data are kept for as long as needed for your enquiry and are then deleted. For this prototype, all data is deleted at the end of the project assessment period unless you ask for earlier deletion.

8. Your rights under UK GDPR

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. How to delete your data

To have your data deleted, send the message DELETE MY DATA in the WhatsApp chat, or email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you used. We will delete your messages and profile and confirm when it is done.

10. Children

StudyPilot is intended for people aged 18 and over.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
